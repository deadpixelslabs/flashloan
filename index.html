Scanner controls

Scanner coverage: exact onchain quotes across Uniswap V2, direct USDG Stock-Token Uniswap V3 pools (0.01% / 0.05% / 0.30% / 1.00%), and discovered Uniswap V4 USDG pools. DEX fees and price impact are already inside the quote. V4 PoolKeys are discovered from a public index, but every V4 amount is re-quoted directly against the official onchain Uniswap V4 Quoter. Scanner gas is provisional. The execution panel checks the complete atomic call and its full gas budget. The scanner groups up to 16 reads into one verified Multicall request, serializes RPC traffic, and caches verified token metadata for 24 hours. A rate limit pauses requests with a visible countdown. The first scan discovers pools; later scans reuse live routes.
